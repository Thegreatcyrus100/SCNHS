About SCNHS

Excellence in Education Since 1969

Santa Cruz National High School (SCNHS) is a prestigious public secondary educational institution located in Santa Cruz, Davao Del Sur. For over five decades, we have been dedicated to providing quality education, shaping future leaders, and building strong communities in Mindanao.

Our Senior High School department offers both Academic and Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) strands, equipping every learner with the skills, knowledge, and character needed for college, employment, and entrepreneurship.

🎯 Mission

To provide quality, inclusive, and relevant education that nurtures academic excellence, life skills, and moral values in every learner.

👁 Vision

A leading public school producing globally competitive, values-driven graduates who contribute meaningfully to society and the nation.

📋 Enrollment Process

1

Submit Requirements

Prepare and submit all required documents to the SHS Registrar's Office or upload via the online portal.

2

Strand Assessment & Interview

Undergo a brief strand assessment and interview to determine the most suitable academic track for you.

3

Enrollment Confirmation

Receive your Section assignment and Learner Reference Number (LRN). Sign the enrollment form and school policies.

4

Receive School Materials

Claim your DepEd-issued modules, textbooks, and school ID during the designated distribution schedule.
